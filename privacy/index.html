BoltVault Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

In short

BoltVault has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. Your collection is kept on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. It's never sent to us — we don't run a server and can't see it.

Where your collection lives

Your collection

Cards, prices paid, folders and your release-calendar edits are stored on your iPhone.

iCloud

With iCloud on for BoltVault, your collection and a few settings sync to your other devices through your own private iCloud account. Apple keeps that copy for you; we can't see it. Turn it off in the Settings app › your name › iCloud.

Backups

A backup you make is saved where you choose in Files. Before a restore, a copy of your collection is saved to Files › BoltVault.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read cards, on your iPhone. Pictures you add to a card stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders and price alerts are worked out and sent by your iPhone itself.

What BoltVault looks up online

Card details and prices

To look a card up, BoltVault asks TCGdex for it by name or number, and gets card pictures from TCGdex.

Exchange rate

Once a day it fetches the euro → pound rate from Frankfurter.

UK shop prices

It searches Total Cards' public shop by card name.

Sealed products

A box's barcode is read on your iPhone and never sent anywhere. Its UK price is looked up on Total Cards by the name you give it.

These requests contain only what's being looked up — never who you are or what else you own. Like any website, those services can see your internet address when they reply.

Shop links

Shop buttons open the shop's own website in your browser. Some links are affiliate links, so the shop knows you came from BoltVault; from then on their own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date, and in the app under Settings › Privacy.

Contact
